# Team LLM Wiki Overview

This wiki is the maintained synthesis layer for team LLM experiments, sources, model notes, feature work, and benchmark outcomes.

Raw evidence lives under `raw/`. Ingest packets promote selected evidence into stable wiki pages through review-aware automation. LLM-assisted synthesis is review-required and must preserve raw provenance, claim boundaries, and claim statuses.
